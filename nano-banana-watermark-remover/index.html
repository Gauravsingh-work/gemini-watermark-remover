How to use it

Open the image tab, choose the Nano Banana preset if it matches, and refine the preview.

Supported media

PNG, JPG and WebP. File support and processing performance vary by browser and device.

How watermark reconstruction works

Compatible visible logos may be applied as semi-transparent overlays. Clearframe uses a configurable mask to estimate and reverse that blending. It is not guaranteed to reconstruct every original pixel, particularly where the overlay or compression destroys information.

Privacy and processing

The processing engine operates in your browser. Files are not intentionally uploaded to a Clearframe processing server. Third-party resources such as fonts or icon libraries may still be requested by the website.

Common questions

Is it free?

Yes. The workspace does not require an account or subscription.

Will it always remove a watermark perfectly?

No. Results depend on mask alignment, watermark opacity, compression and the specific model output.

Can I use it for my own content?

Use it only on media you have permission to edit, and respect applicable platform terms and attribution requirements.
